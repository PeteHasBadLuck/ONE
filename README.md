# ONE

**Dating, simplified. Pay €1 once. Full access.**

ONE is an open-source dating app concept built around honest intentions, unrestricted discovery, privacy, and safety.

## Principles
- One-time €1 membership concept; no subscriptions, premium tiers, boosts, paid visibility, or advertising.
- Adults (18+) only.
- Honest intentions and user-controlled discovery.
- Privacy and safety are core product requirements.
- Open development and transparent governance.

## Current status
The UI is at the prototype stage. The sign-up/sign-in screen is a visual mock-up only. Authentication, database, photo storage, real messaging, payments, and production security are not implemented. Do not use it to collect real profiles or sensitive personal information.

## Funding
The intended model is a €1 one-time payment for full access. Donations and grants may support the project but must never affect matching or visibility. Long-term operating costs and legal/tax treatment must be validated before accepting payments.

## Payment page
A static contribution page preview is available at `payment.html`. The current concept requires a minimum €1 contribution to create a profile. Because the contribution is mandatory, do not assume calling it a donation removes legal, tax, consumer, or refund obligations; get qualified local advice before launch. Checkout is not connected. Do not collect card details or claim payments are active until secure hosted checkout and server-side verification are implemented.
